**The committed direction — "roast ledger":**

- **Ground:** `#f7f2e8`, unbleached paper rather than white — warmth from the material, not from decoration.
- **Ink:** `#2a211c`, a roast brown standing in for black, so the darkest thing on the page is still coffee-coloured.
- **Accent:** `#0d7c8a`, a verdigris teal that reads like a stamped date. It sits on the blue–yellow axis, well away from the warm brown ink, so it stays separable for anyone who cannot split red from green.
- **Muted:** `#6b5d52`, a warm grey-brown for labels — holds legibility on the cream without introducing a fourth family of hue.
- **Pairing:** `jetbrains-mono` display against `source-serif-4` body — utilitarian terminal type for origins, dates and batch labels, humanist print serif for the growers and the roast. Contrast of era and of purpose; neither face is doing the other's job.
- **Archetype:** `dense_index` — the page is the roast log read top to bottom, no marketing hero, no cards. Paired with `technical_spec_sheet` video so the 48-hour claim is carried by rules and figures rather than steam-rising b-roll.
- **Motion:** *measured settle, hard cuts.*
